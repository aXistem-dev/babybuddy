# Milk Stash

The milk stash keeps track of stored breast milk: how much there is, how old it
is, and which milk to use first.

## Putting milk in

When adding a pumping entry, switch on **Into the milk stash** to store its
amount. The milk goes into the stash at the pumping's end time.

Milk that didn't come from a pumping entry, such as milk already in the
freezer, can be added from the **Milk stash** page with **Add milk**.

## Taking milk out

When adding a bottle feeding of breast milk or fortified breast milk, switch on
**From the milk stash**. The bottle's amount leaves the stash at its start time,
oldest milk first. Only bottles with an amount can come from the stash.

Milk that is thrown away is taken out with **Discard milk**, oldest first.

## The Milk Stash page

The page shows the balance and the stored milk, oldest first, with when each
part expires:

- **Fresh**: younger than the warning age.
- **Expires soon**: older than the warning age (48 hours by default).
- **Expired**: older than the maximum age (72 hours by default).

Both ages can be changed in the site settings. When some milk has expired,
**Throw away expired milk** opens a discard entry with the expired amount
filled in.

When more milk was taken out than was put in, the balance shows below zero.
Adding the missing milk corrects it: the next milk put in makes up the
shortfall first.

The stash is worked out from the entries, so editing or deleting a pumping
entry, a bottle or a stash adjustment corrects every figure.

A **Milk stash** card appears on the dashboard once the stash has been used.
