# Stored Milk

Stored milk keeps track of expressed breast milk kept for later: how much there
is, how old it is, and which milk to use first.

## Storing milk

When adding a pumping entry, switch on **Store this milk** to store its amount.
The milk is stored at the pumping's end time.

Milk that didn't come from a pumping entry, such as milk already in the
freezer, can be added from the **Stored milk** page with **Add milk**.

## Using stored milk

When adding a bottle feeding of breast milk or fortified breast milk, switch on
**From stored milk**. The bottle's amount is taken at its start time, oldest
milk first. Only bottles with an amount can come from stored milk.

Milk that is thrown away is taken out with **Discard milk**, oldest first.

## The Stored Milk page

The page shows the balance and the milk in storage, oldest first, with when
each part expires:

- **Fresh**: younger than the warning age.
- **Expires soon**: older than the warning age (48 hours by default).
- **Expired**: older than the maximum age (72 hours by default).

Each stored part links to the pumping entry or stored milk adjustment it came
from. Below it, **Recent changes** lists the last ten movements (pumping,
bottles, added and discarded milk), each linked to its entry.

Both ages can be changed in the site settings. When some milk has expired,
**Throw away expired milk** opens a discard entry with the expired amount
filled in.

When more milk was used than was stored, the balance shows below zero. Adding
the missing milk corrects it: the next milk stored makes up the shortfall
first.

Stored milk is worked out from the entries, so editing or deleting a pumping
entry, a bottle or a stored milk adjustment corrects every figure.

A **Stored milk** card appears on the dashboard once milk has been stored.
